Residential apartment requirements (example specification)

1. Every bedroom shall have a floor area of not less than 9 m².
2. No bedroom shall be narrower than 2.4 m.
3. The kitchen shall share a wall with the living room.
4. A bathroom shall not open directly into a kitchen.
5. Every room shall be accessible through at least one door.
6. Every bedroom shall open directly onto the hall.
7. The dwelling shall contain at least one bathroom.
8. From any point inside any room, the walking distance to an exit door shall not exceed 15 m.
